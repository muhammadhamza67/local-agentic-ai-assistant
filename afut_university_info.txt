University Overview: Al-Fareed University of Technology

Al-Fareed University of Technology (AFUT) is a private university founded in 2005, located in Sahiwal, Punjab, Pakistan. It is known for its focus on engineering, computer science, and applied sciences programs. The university currently has around 6,200 enrolled students across undergraduate and graduate programs.

Leadership
The current Vice Chancellor is Dr. Imran Chaudhry, who took office in 2021. The Dean of the Faculty of Computer Science is Dr. Sana Farooqi. The Registrar's Office is headed by Mr. Tariq Mehmood.

Faculties and Departments
AFUT is organized into four faculties:
1. Faculty of Computer Science and Software Engineering
2. Faculty of Electrical and Electronics Engineering
3. Faculty of Business and Management Sciences
4. Faculty of Applied Sciences (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry)

The Faculty of Computer Science is the largest, with about 2,400 enrolled students, and offers degrees in Computer Science, Software Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence.

Admissions
Undergraduate admissions open twice a year, in Spring (February intake) and Fall (September intake). The minimum requirement for the Computer Science program is 60% marks in Intermediate (FSc/ICS) or equivalent, plus a passing score on the AFUT Entry Test, which covers mathematics, English, and general reasoning.

Tuition fees for the BS Computer Science program are approximately PKR 145,000 per semester, with a one-time admission fee of PKR 25,000. AFUT offers a merit scholarship covering up to 50% of tuition for students scoring above 90% in their entry test.

Campus and Facilities
The main campus spans 45 acres and includes three academic blocks, a central library with over 40,000 books, a dedicated AI and Robotics Lab established in 2023, a sports complex, and on-campus hostels for both male and female students with a combined capacity of 1,200 students.

Notable Programs
In 2023, AFUT launched a specialized BS in Artificial Intelligence program, one of the first dedicated AI undergraduate degrees in the region. The program includes coursework in machine learning, robotics, and natural language processing, along with a mandatory final-year industry capstone project.

The university also runs the AFUT Incubation Center, founded in 2022, which has supported 34 student startups as of 2025, primarily in software and AgriTech sectors.

Academic Calendar
Each academic year is divided into two main semesters (Fall and Spring) plus an optional Summer semester for makeup or accelerated courses. Final exams are held in the last two weeks of each semester, followed by a two-week grading and result-announcement period.

Student Societies
AFUT has over 20 active student societies, including the AFUT Computing Society, the Robotics Club, the Entrepreneurship Society, and the Debate and Literary Society. The Computing Society hosts an annual hackathon called "CodeSprint AFUT" each November.
